🔑 Google Gemini AI: Setup, Plan & Why You Need It

We don't sell subscriptions or charge for AI tokens. Instead, you connect your own personal Google Gemini key directly to Google's official free tier.

1

Where to get the key? (100% Free)

Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com), log in with any Google account, and click the blue "Create API Key" button. It takes 20 seconds.

2

What plan/tier is this?

This is the Google Free Tier. It provides generous rate limits (up to 15 requests per minute and 1,500 daily requests), which is more than enough to read dozens of manhwa chapters daily. You do not need to link a credit card.

3

Where do I paste it?

In M_Translate, open Settings (⚙) ➔ AI Gemini tab, paste your key, and click Save. The key is stored locally on your PC in config.json and never shared with anyone.

4

What does the AI actually do in the app?

1. Slang & Idiom Breakdown: Explains manhwa insults, archaic royal titles, comic sound effects (SFX), and character tone beyond dry dictionary words.
2. Soundscore Composer: Scans chapter panels to generate an adaptive sound_timeline.json, matching character moods and locations with Foley audio.

💡 Language of Explanations: In settings, you can choose whether Gemini answers and explains nuances in English, Ukrainian, or Russian.
🎧 Adaptive 3-Layer Sound Engine & Scene Recognition

Standard readers either play dead silence or loop a boring 3-minute MP3 that ruins the mood during battle scenes. Our sound engine treats your manhwa like a movie soundtrack:

🎼 Layer 1: Melody & Mood (BG)

The emotional theme: a melancholy cello during a tragedy, gentle flutes in a quiet forest, or rising orchestral battle drums. Spans smoothly across 5–15 panels without sudden jarring cuts.

🌲 Layer 2: Scene Ambience (AMB)

Physical space acoustics: water drops in damp dungeons, crackling hearth fire in taverns, howling mountain blizzards, or crowd whispers in royal ballrooms.

⚔️ Layer 3: Dynamic Action & Loops (SFX)

Action cues: sword parries, magic fire blasts, monster roars, frantic heartbeats, or horse gallops that silence naturally as soon as combat ends.

⚡ Zero-Lag Cache Hit (0.001s):

The Gemini AI only analyzes each chapter once. It writes a lightweight sound_timeline.json directly in the chapter folder. The next time you open that chapter, the sound engine boots in less than 1 millisecond with zero internet connection needed.

🗂️ Anki & AnkiConnect: What It Is & Why It's Built-In

Anki is the gold standard for spaced-repetition flashcards. But making manhwa cards manually (copying words, sentence context, screenshotting character art) takes 1–2 minutes per card. M_Translate does it in 2 seconds without switching windows.

1

Step 1: Install Anki on PC

Download the official Anki application from apps.ankiweb.net. It's completely free for Windows.

2

Step 2: Install the official AnkiConnect plugin

In Anki, click Tools ➔ Add-ons ➔ Get Add-ons..., paste code 2055492159, and press OK. Restart Anki and leave it running in the background.

3

Step 3: Instant 1-Click Cards

When reading, press Alt + W on a sentence or Alt + D to crop the character's face. M_Translate automatically builds a stylish Dark Fantasy card with the word, sentence context, and a 16:10 art panel in your "англ манхва" deck.

📌 No Anki running? No problem: If Anki is closed, M_Translate saves your cards locally into an offline SQLite database and generates an importable angl_manhwa.apkg deck file for you.

🛡️ Strict Local Privacy & Our Philosophy
🔒 100% Offline OCR (No Screen Spying):

The neural text recognition engine (RapidOCR) runs entirely offline on your computer's CPU. When you snip a word or dialogue bubble, the screenshot is processed purely in RAM and never leaves your computer. We do not run background servers that collect your screen data.

🧹 Clean Portable Footprint:

Zero Windows registry modification, zero background telemetry services, zero bloat. If you move or delete the app folder, no traces remain on your system.
